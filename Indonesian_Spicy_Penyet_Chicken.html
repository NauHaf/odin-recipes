Indonesian Spicy Penyet Chicken

Description

Ayam penyet (Javanese for squeezed fried chicken) is an Indonesian — more precisely East Javanese cuisine — fried chicken dish consisting of fried chicken that is squeezed with the pestle against the mortar to make it softer, and is served with sambal, slices of cucumbers, fried tofu, and tempeh (mostly cucumber). In Indonesia, penyet dishes such as fried chicken and ribs are commonly associated with Surabaya, the capital city of East Java. The most popular ayam penyet variant is ayam penyet Suroboyo.[2] Ayam penyet is known for its spicy sambal, which is made with a mixture of chilli, anchovies, tomatoes, shallots, garlic, shrimp paste, tamarind and lime juice. Like its namesake, the sambal mixture is then smashed into a paste to be eaten with the dish. Today ayam penyet is commonly found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei and Singapore. It has recently surged in popularity across Southeast Asia, where various chains of franchises have opened selling the dish along with other Indonesian delicacies.

How to make Indonesian Spicy Penyet Chicken

Ingredients:

  • 4 pieces of chicken (drumsticks or thighs)
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 shallots, minced
  • 5 red chilies, sliced (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon shrimp paste (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind paste
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. In a pot, bring water to a boil. Add the chicken pieces, salt, white pepper, and turmeric powder. Boil until the chicken is cooked through, about 15-20 minutes. Remove the chicken and let it cool.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the minced garlic and shallots until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced red chilies and shrimp paste (if using). Cook for a few minutes until the chilies are softened.
  4. Add tamarind paste and sugar. Stir well and cook for another 2-3 minutes. Season with salt to taste.
  5. Squeeze the cooked chicken pieces with a pestle against a mortar to make them softer.
  6. Serve the smashed chicken with the spicy sambal on top, along with slices of cucumber, fried tofu, and tempeh.
